# Remove WhatsApp line labels

## Changes
- Remove labels such as “Support Line”, “Main Sales”, “VIP Desk”, and “Broadcast” from App Messages and WhatsApp conversation rows and headers.
- Remove those labels from WhatsApp session management; identify sessions by their masked phone number instead.
- Remove the line-label field when linking a new WhatsApp number while preserving the existing prototype session data internally.
